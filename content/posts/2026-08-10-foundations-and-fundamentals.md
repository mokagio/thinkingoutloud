David Deutsch [conversation](https://curtjaimungal.substack.com/p/david-deutsch-show-notes) with Curt Jaimungal was one of the most interesting I heard, at least in the late 2025 to mid 2026 period.

Of all the interesting topics, though, the one I feel most compelled to put to paper first is one of definitions.

David explained the difference between how he uses _foundations_ and _fundamentals_ in the context of research.

**Foundations** are the principles underlying a given field.
Think general relativity and quantum mechanics for physics, and whatever explanation will be created to reconcile the two.

**Fundamentals** are those areas of research that give us the tools to bring every field forward.
Think epistemology and constructor theory.

I suspect we need better names for those directions, because in everyday use foundations and fundamentals are synonyms.

But names aside, it's invigorating to have attention brought toward these directions of inquiry.

All progress is good.
Incremental progress should be pursued and celebrated in its own right (see Matt Ridley's [_How Innovation Works_](https://www.amazon.com/How-Innovation-Works-Flourishes-Freedom/dp/0062916602)).
Yet what excites me the most is the thought of smart people working on new tools for thought, and the unpredictable opportunities they will unlock.
