title: 'Two YouTubers Worth Subscribing To'
---

I discovered two YouTubers recently and enjoyed working through their videos.

**[Mo Bitar](https://www.youtube.com/@atmoio)** offers satirical takes on the tech industry and the current [AI hype](https://www.giolodi.com/p/beware-the-ai-apocalypse-prophecies) in particular.
Comedic on the surface, they actually contain sharp observations on the trajectory of the industry and what it means for those who care about craft and quality.

**[Braeden Alberti](https://www.youtube.com/@braedenalberti)** shares detailed critiques of modern media, especially Marvel movies.
Some might consider his work nitpicky, but I appreciate how he voices the disappointment longtime comic-book fans feel as they watch beloved characters bastardized and classic stories, originally complex and profound, discarded in favor of cheap gags and "subverting expectations."
